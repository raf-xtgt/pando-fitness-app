## Health screening

The safety check links to the official **PAR-Q+** (The Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire for Everyone, 2025 edition) at https://eparmedx.com/. Pando does not reproduce the questionnaire: its terms require the entire, unchanged form and written consent for electronic use. Pando stores only the walker's own result (none / some apply), the date and the edition.

The Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire for Everyone (PAR-Q+) and Electronic Physical Activity Readiness Medical Examination (ePARmed-X+). Health & Fitness Journal of Canada 4(2):3-23, 2011.
